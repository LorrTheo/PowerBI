LawnService

A lawn service requested a basic PowerBI report to illustrate their seasonal sales activity for the year 2025 only.  After reviewing the data, I noted to the client that their underlying data had some issues related to categories.  "Grub" was set up correctly with split categories distinguishing "waves".
The others were not set up for these distinguishing waves, which through off some of the results.  The report page "ResearchChecks" shows the issue clearly highlighted.

Names have been obfuscated out of respect, even though no identifying context is available.
