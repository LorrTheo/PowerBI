OccHealth_ATC_Utilization

This was a project that spanned 2020 to 2024, logging and analyzing Certified Athletic Trainer (LAT,ATC) activities within an Industrial site.

Types of activities included clinical, Wellness, and Safety.  The industrial site held approximately 300 regular employees, the ATC was onsite full-time providing services.

all PII or HIPAA content has been scrubbed.
